Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026 · Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Squish Cube for iOS. The app does not require an account. Some information is stored only on your device, while third-party services such as Google AdMob and Apple may process information as described below.

1. Who We Are

Squish Cube is developed and published by Maksym Kokhaniuk (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Privacy contact: maxkokhanuk69@gmail.com.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to the Squish Cube iOS application and to the legal pages on this website. It explains what information may be processed, why it may be processed, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

3. Information Handled by Squish Cube

3.1 Information stored locally on your device

Squish Cube stores gameplay and preference information locally so the game can remember your progress and settings. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • best score and gameplay progress;
  • in-game currency balances, including Shards and Cores;
  • cosmetics, themes, trails, landing effects, and other unlocked or selected items;
  • daily mission, event, and tutorial progress;
  • audio and other game settings;
  • a leaderboard nickname, locally generated player identifier, and best score if you choose to use the leaderboard feature;
  • App Store transaction identifiers used locally to help prevent the same in-app purchase from being credited more than once.

In the current version, this gameplay information is stored on your device and is not transmitted by Squish Cube to a developer-operated server. Removing the app or clearing its local data may remove this information, subject to iOS and App Store behavior.

3.2 Information you provide directly

Squish Cube does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or email address inside the app. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you include in your message and use it to respond to your request.

3.3 Advertising information processed by Google AdMob

Squish Cube uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including rewarded advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, diagnostic or performance information, and device/app information. The exact information processed can vary based on your region, consent choices, device settings, Google Mobile Ads SDK configuration, and the advertising partners involved.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.4 Purchases processed by Apple

Squish Cube may offer in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Payment and billing information is processed by Apple, not by us. The app may receive transaction-related information from StoreKit that is necessary to verify and deliver an in-app purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3.5 Website hosting information

These legal pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit this website, GitHub may process standard technical information associated with serving a webpage, such as IP address and request information, under GitHub's own privacy terms. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Why Information Is Used

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • to operate the game and save local progress and preferences;
  • to deliver virtual items and in-app purchases;
  • to show rewarded and other advertising where enabled;
  • to measure, secure, diagnose, and improve advertising services;
  • to comply with applicable legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, and platform requirements;
  • to answer support or privacy requests that you send to us.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Privacy Choices

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and AdMob privacy messaging features where applicable. In regions where consent or an opt-out choice is required, you may be shown a privacy message before certain advertising processing occurs.

Depending on your location and choices, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise restricted. Where required, you can review or change available advertising privacy choices from: Squish Cube → Settings → Privacy Choices.

You can also use relevant iOS privacy controls. Changing consent or device settings may affect the types of ads you receive but does not remove the core gameplay functionality.

6. Rewarded Advertising

Squish Cube may let you voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as continuing a run or receiving another gameplay reward. The reward is provided only after the advertising SDK reports that the qualifying reward event occurred. Rewarded ads are optional.

7. Legal Bases Where Data Protection Laws Apply

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss data protection law, or similar laws apply, processing may rely on consent where legally required, performance of a requested service, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests that do not override your rights. Google and other advertising partners may identify their own legal bases for processing within the consent interface or their respective privacy notices.

8. How Information May Be Shared

We do not operate a business that sells your directly provided personal information. Information may nevertheless be processed or shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Google and advertising partners, to provide and measure ads in accordance with your available consent choices and applicable law;
  • Apple, to process App Store purchases and platform services;
  • GitHub, to host these legal webpages;
  • legal or safety requirements, if disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, investigate abuse, or maintain security.

9. Data Retention

Gameplay and settings data stored by Squish Cube locally generally remain on your device until you change them, reset them through available app features, or remove the app, subject to iOS behavior. Emails you send to us may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, or comply with legal obligations.

Third-party services retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

10. International Processing

Third-party providers such as Google, Apple, and GitHub operate globally. Information they process may be handled in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Squish Cube does not require users to create an account or directly submit contact information to play. We do not knowingly ask children to send us personal contact information through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us at maxkokhanuk69@gmail.com.

Advertising requirements can differ when an app is directed to children or when a user's age is known to be below an applicable threshold. We configure advertising in accordance with the audience and legal requirements applicable to the released version of the app.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, or the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Because the current version does not maintain a developer-operated account or server profile for gameplay data, much of the game's information is under your control on your device. For advertising choices, use the in-app Privacy Choices option where available. For requests concerning information you have sent directly to us, email maxkokhanuk69@gmail.com.

Requests relating to data independently controlled by Google, Apple, or GitHub may need to be submitted directly to the relevant provider.

13. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app to reduce unauthorized access or misuse. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Squish Cube changes, when we add or remove third-party services, or when legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
